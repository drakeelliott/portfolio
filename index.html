About

I'm a mechanical engineer currently working in industry, with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from MIT. My experience spans research labs, startups, and engineering project teams, building everything from assistive devices to consumer products and fluid systems.

What I enjoy most is owning the full design cycle: taking an idea from first sketch through CAD and analysis, building it, testing it, and iterating on what the hardware teaches me until it works.
